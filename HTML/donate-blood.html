Blood Donation Guide

Everything you need to know before your first or next donation.

What is blood donation?

Blood donation is a voluntary procedure where a person allows blood to be drawn for transfusions or further processing.

Types of donation

Whole Blood

The most common donation type, used directly or separated into components.

Plasma

The liquid portion of blood, often needed for trauma and burn patients.

Platelets

Important for patients receiving cancer treatment and some surgeries.

Double Red Cells

Two units of red cells collected in one visit for eligible donors.

General donor eligibility

  • Typically age 18–65, subject to local guidelines.
  • Generally in good health with no active fever or infection.
  • Required waiting periods may apply after surgery, tattoo/piercing, or illness.
  • Some medicines and vaccinations can cause temporary deferral.
Important: Final eligibility is determined by qualified medical professionals and the blood bank's applicable guidelines.
