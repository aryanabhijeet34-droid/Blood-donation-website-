KNOW YOUR BLOOD GROUP

Blood Group Compatibility

Learn about general red blood cell donation compatibility.

🩸

Why Is Compatibility Important?

Blood groups are classified using the ABO and Rh systems. Compatibility is important because incompatible red blood cell transfusions can cause serious reactions.

The information here is educational only. Medical professionals must perform blood typing and crossmatching before any transfusion.

General Red Blood Cell Donation Guide

Blood Group Can Donate To
O-All groups
O+O+, A+, B+, AB+
A-A-, A+, AB-, AB+
A+A+, AB+
B-B-, B+, AB-, AB+
B+B+, AB+
AB-AB-, AB+
AB+AB+ only
Medical disclaimer: This chart is a simplified guide for red blood cell donation. It is not a transfusion approval tool. Always follow professional blood bank testing and advice.
LEARN AND SPREAD AWARENESS

Why Is Blood Donation Important?

Understanding the importance of voluntary blood donation can encourage more people to participate.

❤️

Save Lives

Donated blood can support patients who need transfusions during medical treatment.

🤝

Support Others

Voluntary donors help hospitals and blood banks maintain blood supplies.

🌍

Build Awareness

Share accurate information and encourage responsible blood donation.

🏥

Support Healthcare

Blood donations are important for many hospital treatments and medical procedures.

Important Things to Know Before Donating

  • Follow the eligibility requirements of the authorized blood donation centre.
  • Provide accurate health information during the donor screening process.
  • Follow the medical staff's instructions before and after donation.
  • Never donate if medical professionals advise you not to.
